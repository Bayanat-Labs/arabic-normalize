## Distinct word types merged by the lossy rules

Arabic-letter tokens were taken from the raw-safe-v1 output. Each token's base form removes only non-lossy differences (harakat, tatweel, Quranic marks, superscript alef, presentation forms, invisible controls). A base form counts as merged when the policy maps it to the same output as a different base form (for example على and علي under bayanat-norm-v1).

| Policy | Distinct base forms | Merged with another | Share | Tokens | Tokens of merged forms | Share |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| bayanat-norm-v1 | 239288 | 15845 | 6.62% | 20840141 | 5016880 | 24.07% |
| asr-eval-v1 | 239288 | 19659 | 8.22% | 20840141 | 6361564 | 30.53% |

A merge is not always an error: it also joins spelling variants of one word (for example a name written with and without hamza). The shares bound how much of this corpus's vocabulary a lossy rule can confuse.
