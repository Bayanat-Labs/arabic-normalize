## Use

Cleaning without changing any letter, mark, digit or punctuation. The recipe for text_diac columns, for display and for legal text.

## Stages (in order)

| # | Stage | Mode | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | reject_invalid | drop | Remove lone surrogates, control characters other than whitespace, and noncharacters; report them in rejected_spans. |
| 2 | strip_controls | strip | Remove invisible format characters: soft hyphen, word joiner, BOM and similar. |
| 3 | presentation_forms | keep | Keep presentation forms. |
| 4 | ligatures_honorific | keep | Keep them, without a flag. |
| 5 | bidi_zw | strip-except-meaningful | Remove bidi controls and zero-width characters, except a ZWNJ that prevents a join and a ZWJ inside an emoji sequence. |
| 6 | canonical_order | NFC | Apply NFC (canonical composition and mark order) from the pinned UCD 18.0.0 tables; a final NFC pass runs at the end. |
| 7 | tatweel | keep | Keep tatweel. |
| 8 | diacritics | keep | Keep harakat. |
| 9 | quranic_marks | keep | Keep Quranic marks. |
| 10 | superscript_alef | keep | Keep the superscript alef. |
| 11 | alef_variants | keep | Keep alef variants. |
| 12 | alef_maqsura | keep | Keep alef maqsura. |
| 13 | taa_marbuta | keep | Keep taa marbuta, without a flag. |
| 14 | hamza_carriers | keep | Keep ؤ and ئ. |
| 15 | persian_urdu_letters | keep | Keep Persian and Urdu letters (ی ک ہ ھ). |
| 16 | digits | keep | Keep digits as written. |
| 17 | punctuation | keep | Keep punctuation. |
| 18 | whitespace | keep | Keep whitespace as written. |
| 19 | latin_case | keep | Keep Latin letter case. |

## Lossy rules

None: no letter is changed.

Every lossy change is logged with `lossy: true` and listed in `lossy_flags`.

## Limitations

- Removes bidi controls, which can change how mixed right-to-left and left-to-right text is displayed: keep text_raw when exact display matters.
- Keeps presentation forms, tatweel, harakat and every letter.
