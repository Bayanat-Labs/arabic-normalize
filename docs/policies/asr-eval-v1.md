## Use

Scoring only: WER and CER for Arabic speech recognition. Apply the same policy to the reference and the hypothesis and report the policy id and hash with every score (TL-001 ArabicWER).

## Stages (in order)

| # | Stage | Mode | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | reject_invalid | drop | Remove lone surrogates, control characters other than whitespace, and noncharacters; report them in rejected_spans. |
| 2 | strip_controls | strip | Remove invisible format characters: soft hyphen, word joiner, BOM and similar. |
| 3 | presentation_forms | map | Map Arabic presentation forms (U+FB50 to U+FDFF, U+FE70 to U+FEFF) to their NFKC letters, for example ﻻ to لا. |
| 4 | ligatures_honorific | flag | Keep honorific word ligatures such as ﷺ and ﷲ as one character and raise ligatures_honorific.flag. |
| 5 | bidi_zw | strip-except-meaningful | Remove bidi controls and zero-width characters, except a ZWNJ that prevents a join and a ZWJ inside an emoji sequence. |
| 6 | canonical_order | NFC | Apply NFC (canonical composition and mark order) from the pinned UCD 18.0.0 tables; a final NFC pass runs at the end. |
| 7 | tatweel | strip | Remove tatweel (ـ). |
| 8 | diacritics | strip | Remove harakat U+064B to U+0652 (tanween, fatha, damma, kasra, shadda, sukun). |
| 9 | quranic_marks | strip | Remove Quranic annotation marks (explicit list in SPEC.md 4.9). |
| 10 | superscript_alef | strip | Remove the superscript (dagger) alef. |
| 11 | alef_variants | map | Map أ إ آ ٱ to ا (lossy). |
| 12 | alef_maqsura | to-yeh | Map ى to ي (lossy). |
| 13 | taa_marbuta | to-heh | Map ة to ه (lossy). |
| 14 | hamza_carriers | keep | Keep ؤ and ئ. |
| 15 | persian_urdu_letters | keep | Keep Persian and Urdu letters (ی ک ہ ھ). |
| 16 | digits | ascii | Map Arabic-Indic and Extended Arabic-Indic digits to 0 to 9. |
| 17 | punctuation | strip | Replace every punctuation mark with a space. |
| 18 | whitespace | collapse | Collapse whitespace runs to one space and trim both ends. |
| 19 | latin_case | keep | Keep Latin letter case. |

## Lossy rules

- `alef_maqsura.to_yeh`
- `alef_variants.map`
- `taa_marbuta.to_heh`

Every lossy change is logged with `lossy: true` and listed in `lossy_flags`.

## Limitations

- Lossy by design (ة to ه, ى to ي, alef variants): never store its output as a text field.
- Arabic decimal and thousands separators are punctuation, so a number such as ١٬٢٥٠ becomes several tokens.
- Latin letter case is kept.
